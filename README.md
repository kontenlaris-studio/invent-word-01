**Wavegrief** (n.): waving back at a stranger who was waving at someone behind you.

One invented word and a one-line definition (79 characters, cap 140) for make.fun's
"Invent a Word We Need" bounty. The word names the small daily social slip everyone
recognises instantly — the wave you cannot take back.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints the entry and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
